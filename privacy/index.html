CentMemo

Privacy

Last updated November 21, 2026. CentMemo is made by Learning is Change, Inc.

What CentMemo holds

  • Your Apple ID’s email address (or the private relay address Apple gives us if you choose to hide yours), to sign you in with Apple.
  • Your transactions and accounts: date, amount, merchant, the bank's description and category, type, and status, from an Apple Card export you import or, in the iPhone app, from Apple Wallet through FinanceKit when you allow it. Balances and credit limits come only from FinanceKit.
  • What you write: your memos and the values you name.
  • Attachments you choose: a reference to a photo or file on your device, and a copy only if you choose to upload one.

We use this only to show it back to you and, if you connect one, to an AI assistant you choose. We never sell it, never use it for advertising, and never share it with data brokers.

Who handles it

  • Supabase stores your account and data (United States), encrypted at rest.
  • GitHub Pages serves the website. It does not receive your financial data.
  • Apple provides FinanceKit data on your iPhone. Apple does not see what CentMemo stores.
  • Anthropic, only if you connect Claude: when Claude reads your CentMemo, what it reads is sent to Anthropic and handled under Anthropic's privacy policy. Claude can read but never change anything. Nothing is sent to any AI unless you connect it yourself.

Your control

  • Disconnect Claude at any time in Claude's connector settings.
  • Delete any memo by clearing it.
  • Delete your account in Settings. That removes every transaction, memo, value, and attachment record at once. It cannot be undone.
  • On iPhone, turn off FinanceKit access in Settings, Privacy & Security, Financial Data.

Notifications

CentMemo never puts amounts, merchants, or any other transaction detail in a notification.
