Import

Apple Card

In Wallet, open Apple Card, then Card Balance, then Export Transactions (CSV). Or export from card.apple.com.

Venmo

In Venmo, tap Me, then the gear, then Statements. Pick a month and download it, once for each month you want.

PayPal

On paypal.com (the app can't export): open Activity, tap the download icon, choose Custom, pick up to 12 months and CSV. Big reports can take a while; PayPal emails you.

Cash App

On cash.app/account (the app gives PDFs only): open Activity and tap the CSV button next to search.
