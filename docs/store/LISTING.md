## Description

Each purchase made on the Apple Card was done with intention. It is a marker
of something that happened in your life, and it is a story worth telling if it
helps you deepen your understanding of what you value and what you are
"investing" in: experiences with your kids, great meals you have had.   <- Ben, adapted from "Each purchase... (experiences with your kids, great meals that you have had, etc.)"

There are far too many charges on a card that go unexamined.            <- Ben ("There are far too many charges on my card that go unexamined.")

CentMemo shows you the purchases you have not written about yet. For each one,
write the memo: what it was, and what it meant. Name your own values and tag
memos with them, then read your month, or a value, as a story.

- Connect Apple Wallet to bring in Apple Card, Apple Cash, and Savings
  transactions, or import an Apple Card export.
- Write on your iPhone or at centmemo.com; your memos follow you.
- Attach a photo from your library to a purchase. CentMemo keeps a reference,
  never a copy.
- Optional: let your own Claude read your memos and imported purchases, to help
  you understand your money. Claude can read but never change anything, and
  never sees Apple Wallet data.
- No ads, no selling of data, no scores. Delete your account and everything in
  it at any time.
