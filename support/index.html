Bring in your purchases

  • Apple Wallet (iPhone): in CentMemo, open Settings, then Connect Apple Wallet, and choose the accounts to share. Change this any time in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Financial Data.
  • An Apple Card export (iPhone or centmemo.com): in Wallet, open Apple Card, then Card Balance, choose a monthly statement, and Export Transactions as CSV. Then choose Import in CentMemo. Importing the same file again adds nothing twice.

Turning off Apple Wallet access

When you turn off CentMemo's access in iOS Settings, CentMemo deletes the Wallet purchases it synced the next time you open it. Imported exports and the memos on them stay.

Delete your account

Settings, then Delete account. It removes every transaction, memo, value, and photo reference at once, and can't be undone.
