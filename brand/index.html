CentMemo

Brand

CentMemo is for writing down what a purchase meant. The brand is a pencil on paper: one circle drawn around what still needs you, and a serif voice for the words people write.

The mark

A sanguine cent sign, circled once in pencil in the person's color: the same gesture the app uses for what still needs a memo. The circle is drawn, never typeset, and runs a little past its start, the way a hand closes a circle.

No brand uses any of these colors with sanguine; each was checked against 4,160 brands.

Type

Newsreader for titles and for the memos people write (New York, the system serif, on Apple devices). The system face for everything else. Money in tabular figures. No handwriting typefaces, ever.

Rules

  • The page is paper and text is ink. Color is a mark, never a background.
  • The person's color marks only what still needs a memo and the values they name.
  • Sanguine is for titles, actions, and drawn marks.
  • Spending is ink, never red. Credits are green.
  • The full rules are in the project's design document (docs/DESIGN.md).
